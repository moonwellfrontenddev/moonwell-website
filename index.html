How donation titles work

Verified, linked one-time USD tips add up across your account: $1 earns Supporter, $5 earns Patron, and $20 earns Benefactor.

When donation tracking is available in-game, open Chat > Donation titles, refresh your details, and copy your account code into the message on every Ko-fi tip. Refresh after payment; visiting Ko-fi alone grants nothing.

Choose an earned title or no title in Settings > Chat title. Memberships, shop orders, commissions, and non-USD payments do not count toward titles.
